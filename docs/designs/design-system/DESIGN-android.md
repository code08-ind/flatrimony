## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant, modern matrimonial and shared-living matching platform. The visual tone combines energetic optimism with emotional warmth, blending playful contemporary dynamics with structural clarity. Designed to evoke excitement, trust, and intimacy, the aesthetic rejects clinical corporate matchmaking in favor of an eclectic, fresh, lifestyle-forward design movement. High-contrast color pairings, pill-shaped geometries, and warm organic canvas surfaces yield an inviting, editorial-yet-accessible digital environment.

## Colors

The palette establishes an expressive, high-impact light mode experience grounded in tactile warmth:

- **Primary (`#4A2FBD` - Jamun Purple):** The anchor of the brand. Used for core interactive elements, key call-to-actions, brand marks, and high-emphasis states.
- **Secondary (`#D7F26B` - Lime):** High-contrast energetic accent. Deployed for badges, highlights, alert pills, promotional banners, and visual contrast against deep purple surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#C2185B` - Warm Rose/Pink):** Heartfelt emotional tone. Reserved for compatibility markers, mutual likes, celebratory tags, and secondary action highlights.
- **Neutral / Canvas (`#F5F2EA` - Warm Natural Cream):** The base background surface, providing an earthy, editorial counterpoint to the vivid chromatic accents without the sterility of pure white.
- **Text & Foreground (`#17141F`):** Deep ink-charcoal used for maximum legibility across body text, headers, and crisp structural iconography.

## Typography

The design system relies entirely on Plus Jakarta Sans to deliver a friendly, human, yet meticulously structured typographic voice. Tight negative tracking on display scales emphasizes punchiness and editorial impact, while natural spacing and generous line heights on body text maintain clean readability across dense profile narratives, lifestyle requirements, and communicative layouts.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column fluid grid orchestrates desktop viewports (collapsing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile). Generous outer canvas margins protect the warm natural cream background, reinforcing breathability and space around cards and content blocks. 

Vertical spacing follows an 8pt modular rhythm. Profile browsing experiences adapt from a dual/triple column layout on desktop screens to focused single-card full-bleed viewports on mobile devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via tonal layering, subtle chromatic tinting, and crisp separation:
- **Base Canvas:** The default tone `#F5F2EA`.
- **Raised Surfaces & Cards:** Clean surfaces lifted using soft ambient drop shadows tinted lightly with Jamun Purple: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(74, 47, 189, 0.08)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Dialogs:** `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(23, 20, 31, 0.12)`.
- **Borders & Outlines:** Low-contrast borders (`rgba(23, 20, 31, 0.08)`) define boundaries without producing harsh, mechanical grids.

## Shapes

The design system implements a full-pill shape language (`ROUND_FULL` / level 3). Interactive triggers, pill buttons, filter chips, badge containers, and contextual tags default to fully rounded stadium geometries (`rounded-full`). Cards, modal dialogs, and large view containers apply maximum soft curvatures (`rounded-3xl` or `2rem`) to maintain harmony with the pill-dominant aesthetic.

## Components

- **Buttons:** 
  - *Primary:* Jamun Purple (`#4A2FBD`) background, white text, fully rounded (`rounded-full`), padded horizontally with `space-lg`.
  - *Secondary / Accent:* Lime (`#D7F26B`) background with deep text (`#17141F`), used for high-urgency or affirmative actions.
  - *Tertiary:* Warm Rose (`#C2185B`) surface or outline for favoriting, matching, or expressive responses.
- **Chips & Badges:**
  - Fully rounded pills for hobbies, lifestyle tags, and roommate preferences.
  - Inactive chips feature subtle cream-tinted borders with `#17141F` text; active chips switch to solid `#4A2FBD` or `#D7F26B` fills.
- **Cards:**
  - Profile and listing cards use white or elevated cream backgrounds, generous inner padding (`space-lg`), soft purple-tinted ambient shadows, and `rounded-3xl` corners.
- **Input Fields:**
  - Stadium/pill-style input fields (`rounded-full`) for single-line search and text inputs. Textareas transition to `rounded-2xl`. Backgrounds use semi-transparent white fills with a `1px` border of `rgba(23, 20, 31, 0.12)`, focusing with a 2px Jamun Purple ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Custom pill toggles and circular selectors. Checked states fill with `#4A2FBD` or `#C2185B` with clean white icons.
- **Match Meters & Compatibility Bars:**
  - Pill progress bars utilizing linear gradients moving from `#C2185B` (Warm Rose) through `#4A2FBD` (Jamun Purple) into `#D7F26B` (Lime) for affinity indicators.
