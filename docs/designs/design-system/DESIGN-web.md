## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-trust, culturally contextual flatmate-matching platform tailored for young professionals and students navigating shared living in Delhi NCR. The interface balances the pragmatic rigor of tenancy logistics with the warmth and optimism of contemporary urban Indian lifestyle products. It consciously avoids generic corporate real estate tropes as well as romantic dating signifiers, leaning instead into an editorial, curated housemate matchmaking language.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Modernism** with dynamic, playful micro-moments. It pairs generous, pill-centric geometry with crisp architectural whitespace, elevated bento layouts, and tactile card stacks. Tactile, soft surfaces feel human and welcoming, while punchy accents provide clear transactional clarity.

- **Tone & Mood:** Warm, assured, reliable, vibrant, culturally resonant without kitsch.
- **Audience:** Gen Z and young millennial urbanites, corporate relocators, and university graduates across Gurgaon, Noida, South Delhi, and North Campus.
- **Design Metaphor:** The "Flatmate Biodata" evolved—clear lifestyle parameters, verified government backing, and aesthetic co-living compatibility.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the organic warmth of a milk-infused tea tone, contrasted against the electric depth of South Asian botanicals and modern textiles.

- **Background Canvas (`#F5F2EA`):** Warm matcha milk. Soft, non-glare, comforting, serving as the foundational paper layer.
- **Surface Layer (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white used for elevated profile cards, sheets, and modular search tiles to generate crisp spatial separation.
- **Primary Text (`#17141F`):** Deep aubergine-ink. Grounded and human, eliminating the harshness of pure black while guaranteeing WCAG AAA compliance.
- **Secondary Text (`#5E584A`):** Muted stone-olive. Reserved for metadata, lifestyle traits, and supporting microcopy.
- **Primary Accent — Jamun Purple (`#4A2FBD`):** Primary action buttons, primary nav selections, and verified profile highlights. Communicates modern authority and focus.
- **Secondary Accent — Matcha Lime (`#D7F26B`):** High-energy signal color strictly applied as a solid fill under `#17141F` text. Reserved for algorithmic compatibility scores (e.g., "94% Match"), selected filter pills, live notification badges, and celebration spark elements.
- **Tertiary Accent — Lavender (`#B9A4FF`):** Tinted container background for lifestyle bento tiles, house rules sections, and informational callouts, strictly paired with `#17141F` text.
- **Action Accent — Rishta Pink (`#C2185B`):** Isolated strictly to affirmative shortlisting actions (Like/Heart states, mutual connection toasts). Never used for general system alerts.
- **Trust Accent — Verified Green (`#1B7F4B` on `#E6F5EC`):** Exclusively reserved for government-backed DigiLocker identity and employment verification chips. Never refer to national identity numbers or specific scheme names; strictly refer to "DigiLocker Verified".

## Typography

The type system balances expressive geometric impact with uncompromised legibility across density-heavy profile details (rents, house rules, dietary habits, and locality specifics).

- **Headlines & Display:** Space Grotesk brings architectural geometry, punchy curves, and confident rhythm. Used for candidate names, location summaries, rent indicators, and onboarding milestones. Tight tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) keeps large titles cohesive.
- **Body & Metadata:** Noto Sans delivers pristine legibility in multi-line bio snippets, amenities lists, and terms of stay. It performs cleanly across high-DPI smartphone displays and web viewports.
- **Labels, Match Indicators & Chips:** Noto Sans in Medium and Bold cuts through bright fills (such as the Matcha Lime and Lavender badges), guaranteeing instant parsing at glance scale.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt modular grid arranged within a flexible container system that prioritizes swipeable mobile ergonomics while extending gracefully into bento-style multi-column dashboards on desktop.

- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column fluid feed with `1rem` outer canvas padding. Card feeds adopt edge-to-edge feel with `0.75rem` internal gutters. Floating primary navigation and bottom sheets maintain thumb-reachable action zones.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column grid with `1.25rem` gutters. Profile browsing splits into an exploratory list on the left and sticky mini-biodata view on the right.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column structured layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px`. Central feed occupies 7 columns, accompanied by a 5-column persistent compatibility inspector containing lifestyle matrices, neighborhood safety ratings, and DigiLocker badges.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-xl` / `2.5rem`) separate distinct card sections, while micro-attributes (e.g., non-smoker, vegetarian, early riser) pack compactly using `space-xs` and `space-sm` gaps inside horizontal scrolling ribbons.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic, heavy drop shadows in favor of crisp tonal layers, intentional border offsets, and soft diffused ambient occlusions:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat Warm Matcha Milk `#F5F2EA`. No shadow, zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Bento Tiles):** Solid `#FFFFFF`, `#B9A4FF`, or `#E6F5EC` containers placed directly on canvas. Separation is achieved through clean boundary contrast and an ultra-subtle, warm ambient glow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(23, 20, 31, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover Cards & Sticky Bars):** Elevated interaction state. Soft directional shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(74, 47, 189, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays, Floating Action Buttons & Dialogs):** Floating pill actions and verification detail bottom sheets utilize dual-layer depth: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(23, 20, 31, 0.12), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(23, 20, 31, 0.04)`.
- **Ghost Outlines:** Where interactive components require edge distinction against complex photo backdrops, apply a `1px` inner stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)` or `rgba(23, 20, 31, 0.06)`. Never use harsh, dark borders.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft, ergonomic pill-forward shape geometry that communicates approachability, warmth, and modern product design:

- **Full Pill Shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Used universally across primary, secondary, and ghost buttons, compatibility percentage badges, filter tags, text inputs, search pills, and floating action clusters.
- **Standard Profile Cards (`border-radius: 24px`):** All modular containers, lifestyle preference blocks, neighborhood insight tiles, and dialog modals utilize `24px` corners.
- **Immersive Photo Cards (`border-radius: 32px`):** Large hero imagery, flat visual tours, candidate visual portraits, and swipe discovery viewports use an expanded `32px` radius with clipped interior media to create friendly, tactile visual anchors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped, background `Jamun purple` (`#4A2FBD`), text `#FFFFFF`. Hover state features a slight brighten with `2px` scale transition. Focus ring uses `Matcha lime` (`#D7F26B`) at `3px` offset.
- **Match / Compatibility Pill:** Pill-shaped, fill `Matcha lime` (`#D7F26B`), text `Primary ink` (`#17141F`) in Bold. Often paired with a subtle sparkle star icon.
- **Rishta Heart (Like) Button:** Circular pill container (`48px x 48px` or `56px x 56px`), background `#FFFFFF` with soft Level 2 elevation. Icon colored `Rishta pink` (`#C2185B`). When activated, fill turns `#C2185B` with `#FFFFFF` heart icon and quick spring animation.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Button:** Pill-shaped, clear fill with `1px` border of `rgba(23, 20, 31, 0.12)`, text `#17141F`.

### Verification Badges
- **DigiLocker Verified Badge:** Pill-shaped container filled with `#E6F5EC`, displaying `#1B7F4B` text and verified check icon. Copy strictly states `"DigiLocker Verified"` or `"Govt ID Verified via DigiLocker"`. Never mention UID or national identity registration labels.

### Chips & Tags
- **Filter Chips (Default):** Pill-shaped, `#FFFFFF` background, `#17141F` text, `1px` border of `rgba(94, 88, 74, 0.15)`.
- **Filter Chips (Active/Selected):** Pill-shaped, filled with `Matcha lime` (`#D7F26B`), borderless, text `#17141F` (Font Weight 600).
- **Lifestyle Preference Chips (Diet, Work Style, Habits):** Pill-shaped, neutral tone `rgba(23, 20, 31, 0.05)`, text `#5E584A`.

### Cards
- **Discovery Profile Card:** White background `#FFFFFF`, corner radius `24px` (or `32px` when media-dominant), internal padding `1.5rem`. Contains candidate hero imagery, DigiLocker green pill at top right, compatibility score top left, and lifestyle tags along the footer.
- **Bento Tiles:** Modular property or house-rule widgets. Use solid Lavender (`#B9A4FF`) or crisp white backgrounds with `24px` radius and `#17141F` text.

### Input Fields
- Fully pill-shaped search bars (`rounded-full`) with `#FFFFFF` background, height `52px`, left-aligned Lucide search outline icon in `#5E584A`, and placeholder text in `#5E584A`. Focus creates a `2px` outer stroke in `Jamun purple` (`#4A2FBD`).

### Selection Controls
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes:** Circular pill-matched radius. Checked state fills with `Jamun purple` (`#4A2FBD`) with white check or dot indicator. Unchecked borders use `rgba(23, 20, 31, 0.2)`.

### Iconography
- Strict Lucide outline icons (`stroke-width: 1.75px` or `2px`). Rounded line caps and corners that seamlessly echo the pill shape language of the design system.
