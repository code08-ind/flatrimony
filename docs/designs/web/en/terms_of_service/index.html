1. Who can use Flatrimony

Flatrimony is an invite-driven and verified tenant discovery ecosystem built purposefully for shared accommodation across the National Capital Region (NCR). Access to and membership on our platform is conditional upon your strict agreement to and compliance with these foundational eligibility requirements:

  • Minimum Age Requirement: You must be at least 18 years of age to register, review shared flats, or message any cohort member.
  • Strict Intended Use: Flatrimony is designed exclusively for verified flatmate matching, room-sharing logistics, and household co-tenancy compatibility. The platform is not a dating service, matrimonial portal, casual meetup network, or employment board. Soliciting personal romantic encounters or unverified commerce is grounds for immediate termination.
  • Bona Fide Residents Only: Eligibility is restricted entirely to individual bona fide prospective residents or current leaseholding tenants seeking roommate replacements in Delhi NCR.
Strict Zero-Broker Policy Real estate brokers, commercial leasing agencies, property agents, property aggregators, and unauthorized middlemen are categorically forbidden. Any profile suspected of acting as a commercial broker or operating sub-leasing networks will be permanently banned with forfeited paid tokens and zero appeal recourse.

2. Your Account and DigiLocker Verification

To ensure collective physical safety and eliminate anonymous harassment, Flatrimony implements mandatory identity authentication before any member is permitted to transmit direct messages, view detailed residency biodatas, or attend offline property visits.

  • Mandatory DigiLocker Verification: Verification via government-sanctioned DigiLocker integration is mandatory prior to sending connection requests ('Rishtas') or unlocking mutual chat channels.
  • Minimal Data Retention Standard: Flatrimony interfaces strictly through certified, audited verification partners. We retrieve and retain only verified legal full name, date of birth, gender, and a non-reversible cryptographic hash validating document authenticity. We never inspect, store, or warehouse raw national identity documents, biometrics, or identification numbers.
  • Single Identity Rule: Flatrimony strictly enforces a 'One Person, One Account' governance rule. Maintaining burner accounts, creating parallel profiles using third-party credentials, or falsifying your employer or educational biodata will lead to immediate, irrevocable suspension across your device footprint.

3. Listings and True Monthly Cost

Every room posting, flat share invitation, or pre-lease co-application created on Flatrimony must adhere strictly to factual integrity and radical financial transparency.

  • True monthly cost: Every listing must publish the inclusive True Monthly Cost. This mandatory total must account for base room rent, society maintenance charges, regular shared cook and domestic staff dues, Wi-Fi subscriptions, estimated electricity splits, and mandatory clubhouse levies. Omitting fixed overheads or introducing surprise expenses during site visits is a breach of our Community Code.
  • Unedited Video Walkthroughs: Room vacancy postings require real, continuous, and unfiltered video walkthroughs documenting authentic bedroom natural daylight, bathroom water pressure, and true ambient street acoustic levels. Staged real-estate stock renders, architectural mockups, or heavily filtered promotional assets are flagged and purged.
  • Zero Tolerance on Brokerage: No brokerage, finder's cut, sub-lease markup, or administrative fee may ever be demanded, levied, or settled through Flatrimony.

4. Meeting Safely and Payments

Your real-world safety is our utmost structural priority. We establish strict operational guardrails surrounding in-person viewings and tenancy monetary transactions.

'Pehli Mulaqat' Public Meeting Protocol Before committing to a residential flat walkthrough, we strongly recommend that prospective co-habitants host their introductory compatibility interview at vetted public transit corridors or daylight partnership establishments (such as busy public places or vetted transit corridors) utilizing our in-app safety session geofenced check-in.
  • Prohibition of Pre-Visit Financial Transfers: NEVER send advance booking tokens, holding deposits, key reserves, or advance rental payments over UPI or bank wire before executing an in-person physical viewing and reviewing the landlord's registered title deed or primary tenancy agreement. Flatrimony will never prompt you to wire funds to secure a room queue.
  • Pure Matchmaking Intermediary Scope: Flatrimony Technologies Pvt. Ltd. operates exclusively as a software matching intermediary. We do not act as an escrow agent, rent collector, landlord agent, or custodian of rental security deposits.

To maintain peace of mind across our urban shared apartments, our Trust & Safety operations team enforces an escalating sanctions matrix across reported infractions:

Level 1 · Warning

Formal written warning and compliance corrective notice logged on your permanent account record.

Level 2 · Moderation

Removal of offending listing content, lifestyle badge rescission, and algorithmic search shadow-ban.

Level 3 · Suspension
