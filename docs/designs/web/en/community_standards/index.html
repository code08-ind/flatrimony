Delhi NCR Safety Desk
01

Be Real

Authenticity is the bedrock of reliable shared living. Every individual listing a room or browsing for flatmates must maintain a singular, authentic persona backed by verified national credentials.

DigiLocker Verification

To initiate conversations or schedule flat visits, your legal name and age must be corroborated via official government records. Unverified profiles cannot access member contact data.

Recent, Unaltered Imagery

Photos must show you clearly and present real room conditions without deceptive filters or staging. Stock renders, avatars, and pictures of celebrities trigger automated removal.

Privacy: Only your verified first name and verified lifestyle biodata are shown publicly. Surnames, phone numbers, and company affiliations remain masked until you mutually accept an introduction request. Burner accounts are subject to automatic permanent hardware blocks.

02

Flatmates, Not Dating

Flatrimony is engineered purely for shared home tenancy, lease-splitting, and co-living logistics. We are fundamentally not a dating application, matrimonial matchmaker, or casual social mixer.

Strictly Prohibited: Romantic or Sexual Solicitations

Any user sending flirtatious messages, sexual comments, unsolicited compliments on appearance, requests for dates, or attempting to leverage tenancy inquiries for romantic overtures will receive an irreversible, immediate lifetime platform ban.

If an exchange makes you feel uncomfortable or strays beyond flat logistics, house rules, and rent arrangements, use the in-thread "Flag for Romance/Harassment" action. Trust moderators review flagged messages within 120 minutes.

03

No Brokers or Landlords

Our ecosystem is built exclusively by tenants, for tenants. We prohibit commercial intermediaries whose financial incentives distort real housing costs and misrepresent living situations.

No brokers Zero Fees
  • No Brokerage Fees: You will never be asked to pay half a month’s rent, consultation charges, or document fees to anyone on Flatrimony.
  • No Visiting Charges: Demanding petrol fees, key-access fees, or security-gate entry passes prior to a flat tour is an immediate offense.
  • Residents Only: Listings may only be created by an active co-tenant vacating their own room or seeking an incoming flatmate for an ongoing lease.

Property management companies, corporate hostel aggregators, and commercial dealers operating clandestine shell profiles will be identified by algorithmic device tracking and referred to local consumer dispute authorities.

04

Never Ask for Advance Money

The single most common co-living fraud across Gurgaon and Noida involves asking prospective flatmates to transfer “token amounts” or “gate pass security deposits” via UPI before an in-person physical inspection.

Automated Financial Guardrails

Our secure messaging layer automatically detects payment handles (UPI IDs, QR codes, Google Pay links, phone numbers coupled with monetary requests) exchanged prior to a confirmed physical visit checklist. Both parties will see an explicit red warning banner advising against advance settlement.

Always meet in daylight, inspect the original tenancy agreement, and verify landlord permissions before transferring rent or security deposits.
05

Respect & Fair Preferences

Finding a compatible flatmate requires clear lifestyle alignment. We distinguish between reasonable day-to-day household habits and unlawful discriminatory exclusions.

Permitted Objective Preferences
  • • Gender preference for safe shared spaces
  • • Kitchen policies (strictly vegetarian / vegan / non-veg permitted)
  • • Sleep routines (early birds vs. night-shift professionals)
  • • Smoking, alcohol, and house party policies
  • • Pet compatibility and verified allergies
  • • Work-from-home acoustic habits
Strictly Prohibited Bias
  • • Religion or caste requirements
  • • State, linguistic, or regional origin bias
  • • Marital status prejudices
  • • Sexual orientation or gender identity discrimination
  • • Degrading remarks regarding a member's profession
  • • Verbal slurs, insults, or demeaning vocabulary
06

Safety & Protected Spaces

Women moving into Delhi NCR face acute safety considerations. Flatrimony incorporates structural barriers designed to prevent intrusive profiling and maintain inviolable protective bubbles.

Women-Only Enclaves

Flats designated as "All-Women" and profiles of female seekers seeking female roommates are strictly hidden from male accounts. Only users with female government identity corroborated through DigiLocker can view these listings, initiate introductions, or receive location coordinates.

In-app live safety session

When scheduling an on-site room tour, activate safety session. The app creates a temporary live-location audit link shareable with your trusted emergency contacts and offers an instantaneous one-touch SOS beacon that triggers an alert to local support desks.

07

Reporting & Response Times

Account accountability is fast and unbureaucratic. A persistent contextual report button is present within two taps on every bio, card, and message thread across the product.

Priority 1 · Safety Incident
< 2 Hours Response

Physical threats, stalking, romantic harassment, or unsolicited off-platform contact. Reviewed by human trust officers daily from 9:00 AM to 11:00 PM IST.

Priority 2 · Commercial Spam
< 24 Hours Resolution

Suspected property dealers, broker intermediaries, misleading rent amounts, or advance token requests. Involves forensic device cross-checks.

08

Enforcement Escalation Ladder

Except in severe safety breaches (which trigger immediate permanent termination), our trust operations apply an escalating 4-step compliance model:

Step 1
Formal Warning

Account flag logged. Educational notice issued regarding specific clause infraction.

Step 2
Content Removal

Listing delisted, biodata masked from feed, and search shadow-ban activated.

Step 3
7-Day Suspension

Complete platform freeze, instant chat lockout, and removal of pending visits.

Step 4
Permanent Ban

Irreversible hardware hash, phone, and DigiLocker ID blacklist.

Predatory behavior, stalking, and advance fee scams bypass Steps 1–3 and trigger an immediate lifetime ban.
09

Appeals Process

We value procedural justice. If your listing was removed or account restricted in error, you are entitled to submit one formal appeal per disciplinary action.

Independent Review Protocol

Every appeal is reviewed independently by a senior trust moderator who was not involved in the original restriction decision. Reviews are conducted within 72 hours of submission. To request a review, email appeals@flatrimony.in referencing your registered phone number and case token. Decisions issued following appeal review are definitive.
