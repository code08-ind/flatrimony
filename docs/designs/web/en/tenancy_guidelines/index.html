videocam Verified Walkthroughs

Inspect in-app unedited video logs to verify genuine natural light, active water pressure, and room floorplates.

04

Your Rent Agreement

Most residential leases across Haryana, Delhi, and Uttar Pradesh are executed as an 11-Month Leave and License Agreement to bypass protracted tenancy litigation under traditional Rent Control acts.

description Notice & Lock-in Periods

Verify if your contract contains an initial lock-in period (commonly 3–6 months). Ensure the standard mutual notice period is 30 to 60 calendar days with clear communication avenues.

assignment e-Stamping & Attestation

Leases must be executed on verified non-judicial e-stamp paper (value between ₹100 and ₹500 depending on regional state rules in Delhi, Haryana, or UP) and notarized.

When Someone Leaves

Career transitions and relocations are natural. To maintain lease stability, departing tenants must not leave the remaining flatmates financially encumbered.

Flatrimony Replacement Protocol

Departing co-tenants should activate the Flatrimony Replacement Finder at least 30 days prior to their planned exit. The existing tenants retain absolute veto rights over lifestyle alignment, ensuring incoming housemates match the household's cleanliness, culinary, and social parameters before executing lease novation.

08

Getting Your Deposit Back

Reclaiming your capital cleanly requires documentation rather than confrontation. Leverage your original verified record to resolve final settlements.

A Move-Out Audit

Compare room states side-by-side with day-one photo records to disprove arbitrary repainting or wear-and-tear claims.

B Utility Clearance

Procure official final meter readings for DHBVN/UPPCL power and piped natural gas (PNG) to settle accounts to the exact day.

C Written Refund Timeline

Get a signed bank transfer timeline (usually 7 to 14 days post-handover) on the landlord's official WhatsApp or letterhead.
