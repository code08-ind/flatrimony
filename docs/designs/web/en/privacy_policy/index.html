1. What We Collect

Flatrimony adheres to strict data minimization principles. We capture only the information required to calculate lifestyle compatibility matrices, eradicate rental broker infiltration, and uphold roommate personal safety during physical flat inspections across Delhi NCR.

What we never collect
  • No Contact List Scraping: We never request, read, or sync your phone address book or WhatsApp contacts.
  • No Persistent Geolocation Tracking: We do not track your real-time or background coordinates. Coordinates are collected solely on an explicit, one-time manual basis during an activated safety session session.
  • No Banking Credentials: We never capture, store, or process raw credit cards, debit PINs, net banking passwords, or UPI MPINs.

Data points we collect directly from you:

  • Profile & Tenancy Parameters: Sleep timings, AC temperature thresholds, dietary preferences (veg, non-veg, vegan, Jain), work schedules (hybrid, remote, on-site), and preferred Delhi Metro transit stations.
  • Authentication Credentials: Verified 10-digit mobile number and WhatsApp ID exclusively used for cryptographic One-Time Password (OTP) sign-ins.
  • Lifestyle Compatibility Questionnaires: Cleanliness standards, guest policies, pet tolerance, smoking/drinking preferences, and co-living social boundaries.

2. Identity Verification via DigiLocker

To maintain a zero-broker ecosystem and safeguard co-habitants from impersonators, Flatrimony connects to an authorized, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)-licensed DigiLocker partner gateway.

What Flatrimony Retains
  • • Legal First & Last Name
  • • Date of Birth (Age confirmation)
  • • Gender
  • • Irreversible cryptographic verification token hash
What Flatrimony NEVER Stores
  • • National ID numbers or documents
  • • Scanned or original identity PDF files
  • • Biometric data (fingerprint or iris)
  • • Raw PAN, voter ID, or passport numbers

We verify your identity through DigiLocker. We keep your name, date of birth, gender and a verification reference. We never ask for or store your ID number.

4. Where Your Data Lives (India Residency)

In strict compliance with local data localization mandates, all Flatrimony primary relational databases, user profiles, lifestyle biodatas, chat logs, and safety records are hosted exclusively within Indian territorial boundaries:

Primary Indian Data Facilities:Your data is stored in India, in Mumbai: our database runs on Supabase (South Asia – Mumbai region) and our servers run on Google Cloud (Mumbai region).

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

Flatrimony does not operate an open or indexable directory. Personal contact vectors are guarded behind progressive mutual consent barriers:

  • Two-Way Mutual Rishta Consent: Your contact number, complete biodata profile, and exact apartment unit number remain concealed from public view. They are revealed to another seeker solely after both users mutually accept a match request.
  • Designated Emergency Contacts: During active safety session visits or an SOS broadcast, temporary telemetry is transmitted exclusively to the emergency contact configured in your trust preferences.
  • Zero Commercial Data Monetization: We never sell, lease, barter, or distribute your identity or matching biodatas to real estate brokers, advertisers, financial lenders, or telemarketers.

7. Your Rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a Data Principal under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you hold legally enforceable rights over your digital persona:

Right to Summary Access: You can request a clear overview of all biographical, behavioral, and matching parameters held by us.
Right to Correction: Update outdated commute choices, changing job locations, dietary shifts, or incorrect name representations.
Right to Erasure & Forgetting: Request complete digital decommissioning of your biodata once you have secured tenancy.
Right to Consent Revocation: Withdraw permission at any point through the settings dashboard without incurring service penalization.
Right to Data Portability: Obtain a standard machine-readable JSON archive containing all personal records within 7 days of submitting a valid request.

8. Security & Encryption Standards

Flatrimony enforces defence-in-depth protocols across every network layer:

In Transit Enforced TLS 1.3 protocol with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) across all client connections.
At Rest Hardware-accelerated AES-256 bit column-level database encryption for sensitive profile columns.
Internal Access Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) requiring hardware security keys (FIDO2/WebAuthn) for staff.
